## Notes from earlier tasks

- **T-018:** resistances have almost no effect while no weapon is physical and a L1 fireball one-shots a 2-HP slime (fire weakness invisible). Re-check once claws, bite and saw blade exist.
- **T-028:** gold rate `floor(score / 20)` gives ~65 gold for a 300 s, ~1000-kill run, while one armoury track costs 1,100 and all six 6,600. Tune the rate and/or costs so a player buys a first rank after 1–2 runs and fills a track in ~10–15 runs (judgement; say what you chose).
- **T-028:** `?debug=1` runs pay gold (F1–F5 could farm it). Decide whether debug runs should pay.
- **T-019 + its review:** the apparent "fireball knockback costs the aim bot" effect is build divergence, not knockback (see the [T-019 review](../../reports/reviews/2026-11-26-T-019-review.md) finding 4): with greedy picks, any position change reshuffles offers and per-seed tower loss is heavy-tailed. **Judge balance by medians and run-count targets, and test knock/number changes with fixed loadouts** (level-ups suppressed), not by means over picked builds.
- **T-019:** builds with Amount now hold the golem off to the 7:00 cap in up to 5/64 runs (each hit still pushes it 1 px). T-026 (poise) replaces that rule; re-measure after it.
- **T-019:** Might is +20/40/60% (was +25/50/80%).
- **T-027:** the bots never walk to braziers, so their effect on survival is unmeasured; add a brazier-seeking behaviour to the moving bots (break and collect when within some range) before judging the 25 s interval, the 60/25/15 weights and +20 HP. The Duration card text still says only "+25% mini tower life" although Duration also lengthens pickups (fix the text).
- **T-027:** the soak test (seed 7) only requires > 100 kills and swung from 192 to 7,239 kills as the random stream shifted; make its assertion robust to stream shifts.
- **T-024:** stage leads only *peak* in their stage (dominant leads ended nearly every bot run by 3:00); mounted knights are 1–3% of spawns because HP 30 / DPS 15 make them run-enders — tune those stats before raising their weight. Slime share above ~30 at 1:00 kills many runs in Ooze. Stampedes are toothless at horse DPS 4 (1–2 tower HP per run); levers: horse DPS, count, spacing, frequency (`stampede` fields on timeline rows; not in `--set`). aim+greedy median fell 312 → 182 s (bimodal: falls at 1:45–3:00 or reaches the golem).
- **T-023:** 16-seed measure runs are unreliable now (greedy medians swing up to 150 s between runs): use ≥ 48 seeds. Fixed-build probe (fireball L1 + one weapon at L3, no level-ups), median survival: bite 113.6 s, saw 112.9, left/right claw 118.7/119.5, ring 150.0, mini tower 146.3 — bite and saw are buff candidates. With bite and saw in the pool, greedy bots die to slimes about twice as often (both physical; slimes resist 0.5). Buff card texts (Area, Duration, Amount) do not mention the new weapons; Duration still says "mini tower life" only.
- **T-023:** enemies now carry `maxHp` (needed by the bite's execute); anything creating enemies must set it.
- **T-025:** slimes split twice (HP 3/2/1): one spawn is 11 HP over 7 bodies (was 4 over 3). At slime DPS 8 the bots died in Ooze (48 seeds: aim+greedy 173 → 107 s, falls to slimes 13 → 39/48). The orchestrator set slime DPS 4 as an interim (T-025's what-if: kite+greedy 144.8 s); retune properly (levers: per-generation DPS, gen HP, slime weight, speed 25 → 122.6 s). Fusion (> 100 slimes) never happens on the default timeline (median peak 13 alive); reconsider the threshold. Megaslime is effectively a reward (soaks shots, big gem); watch farming. Bite/saw (physical) do poorly vs slimes. `megaslime_hit` sound is unused — candidate for fusion/megaslime hits.
- **T-026 + orchestrator:** golem poise grows ×1.5 per stagger (a fixed threshold let any full build hold it forever). Probe: full builds now fall 19–23 s after the golem with 8–10 staggers. The tower shove (30 px/s while touching) costs the golem ~11% of contact time. Check the shake feel and whether the golem should still be outrunnable by dragging the tower (old D-5, now moot for builds; check the dragging strategy with a bot).
- **T-030:** "Any card" (25 XP) is never affordable at the 1:30 merchant (more than a whole level then) — consider pricing as a share of the current level's XP. Robbing at 1:30 takes ~90 s of focused fire (maybe too tanky). Bots ignore the merchant and leave any shop; add "rob if in range"/"trade when affordable" bot behaviour to measure him. Owner taste point to raise: without aim input (gamepad without right stick) the fireball fires along the walk, so walking up to him shoots him and ends the trade. The debug overlay shows no merchant circle or count.
- **T-020 damage calculator** ([report](../../reports/measurements/2026-11-26-damage-calculator.md); `node tools/dps.mjs`, `--check`): (1) proc stacking dwarfs everything — Explode 3 + Split 3 + Pierce 3 + Area 3 takes a L5 fireball from 20 to 1,812 DPS in a crowd (×90, measured); every ceiling from 3:00 is fireball 5 + mini tower 5 + those buffs at 9–12× pressure; floor and ceiling 8–14× apart because Pierce reaches fireballs only at L3. (2) Mini tower L5 out-shoots the fireball it copies (8.75 vs 5 shots/s). (3) The laser is dominated everywhere (6 DPS single-target at L5 vs fireball 20, bite 18, right claw 12.9). (4) Ooze is a wall: floor covers 0.39–0.62 of pressure at the bots' levels; bots are level 1 at 1:00 because gems drop out of reach (all gems → level 4). (5) Late game trivial and level-ups run out: a full build costs 31 level-ups (level 32, reached ~3:30); collecting every gem would give level 86 by 5:00; the last 90 s offer only Repair. Also: dual claw is the strongest crowd weapon (60.8 DPS at Siege); the bite is weak in crowds (overkill). Models exclude knockback (ring/claw/bite are upper bounds), mage orbs, braziers, golem poise.
- **T-020 review:** the calculator's first report overstates mixed-crowd damage by 8–24% (hit allocation by HP instead of hits-to-kill): the 1:30 "winnable" ceiling is really ~0.91 (unwinnable), and "late game trivial from 3:30" does not hold (5:00 floor ~0.83). Use the corrected tool and report from T-039, not the first report.
- **T-031:** the princess is a real buff for runs reaching 2:00 (kite+greedy reaching 5:00 4/16 → 8/16; with the reward undone 6/16): +25 max HP and a full heal per rescue. Bots ignore her (passive rescues only: 6/12 and 12/17 rescued). `measure.mjs` `repaired` counts only level-up picks; tower max HP is no longer fixed during a run.
- **T-032:** the prince is a net XP gain for the bots (they pick up gems eagerly, so he steals little: biggest sack 12; bounty +5); his effect on survival is within rng noise. He is an enemy type but not in `ENEMY_TYPES` (no bestiary card, always tier 1, no tier sprites). Bots aim at him only via "nearest the tower", which may pull aim from real threats. All his numbers are *unmeasured* (interval 100 s from 135 s, steal range 60, grab radius 120, every 0.5 s, HP 40, speed 70, bounty 5). `dps.mjs` excludes him from pressure (40 HP / 100 s, negligible).
